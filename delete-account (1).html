Delete My Account

⚠️ This action is permanent. Deleting your account will remove all your data and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Your booking history
  • Your reviews and ratings
  • Portfolio images (for stylists)
  • Salon and technician data linked to your account

How to request deletion

Send us an email from the address associated with your MySalon account. We will process your request within 7 business days.
